This co-authored work, presented at NeurIPS 2023, investigates a novel approach to composing parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) modules via arithmetic operations, allowing flexible combination of task-specific adaptations while retaining the efficiency benefits of parameter-efficient learning methods.
